About Our Library

A Learning Space for Every NNSHS Student

The Navotas National Science High School Library serves as a center for learning, research, and academic excellence. It provides students and teachers with quality learning resources, references, research materials, and a quiet place for studying.

Through the Library Management System, users can browse books, borrow materials, monitor library visits, and explore research papers anytime and anywhere.

Meet the Team

NNSHS Library Club Officers

The Library Club Officers are committed to promoting reading, research, and academic excellence within the school community.

Library President

Leads library programs and supervises club activities.

Vice President

Secretary
